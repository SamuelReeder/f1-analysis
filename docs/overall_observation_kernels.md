# Conditional weekend observations

[`overall_observation_kernels.py`](../analysis/overall_observation_kernels.py)
connects the [joint forecast states](overall_forecast_states.md) to observation
kernels from the existing successor likelihood. It supplies development
predictions conditional on explicit scenarios. It does not fit a model, amend
the failed numerical study, score real held-out results or publish a ranking.

The iterator consumes the same posterior mapping for both latent states and
observation coefficients. All arrays retain their chain and draw axes. It
validates the entire observation-parameter inventory before yielding, records
the consumed state and observation identities separately, and preserves every
draw across contiguous batches. The caller must use the checked archive reader,
complete its immutable-source checks and account for every batch before treating
an output as complete. Shape checks and matching array identities alone cannot
establish that a fit converged.

## Qualifying

For each declared elimination segment, the location is the propagated qualifying
total plus a new car-segment deviation shared by teammates. Those car deviations
are centred over the cars still participating in that segment. Their population
scale comes from the same posterior draw as the driver and car states. A fresh
session log-scale innovation supplies the common scale; fresh Student-t residuals
use that draw's degrees of freedom. This preserves the existing qualifying
likelihood's shared effects and heavy tails. The segment intercept cancels from
all comparisons and is not drawn.

Earlier eliminations retain their positions. The generated quantity is qualifying
position conditional on every entrant setting a representative time. The retained
segment observations are transformed relative pace, with the common intercept
omitted; they are not raw lap-time forecasts. Active masks identify which entrants
actually participate in each generated segment.

Positions become grid features only under an explicit scenario that assumes no
penalties and equates the qualifying order with the grid. Under the separate
actual-grid information set, the supplied grid is the covariate. The pre-weekend
posterior is not reweighted by simulated qualifying in that race-stage test.
This follows the model's conditional race likelihood; it does not by itself
validate the end-to-end generated grid.

## Race context, order and completion

Known-circuit coefficients use the likelihood's training-catalogue centring of
circuit deviations, with the supplied wet and neutralisation indicators. Grid
and pace coefficients are exponentiated; the hazard uses the logistic link.
Hazard calculations retain log probabilities directly so finite extreme logits
do not turn a possible failure into an impossible one through early rounding.
Arithmetic overflow or unusable underflow in the pace/grid coefficient rejects
the prediction instead of clipping it.

Race strength combines the same draw's total race state, pace coefficient and
log-grid term. Independent Gumbel perturbations generate a Plackett–Luce order
on the running panel. The likelihood helper evaluates the exact conditional
order probability, including empty and singleton panels. An entrant outside that
panel has a zero position; this is not a final classification or a points score.
The calculation uses relative suffix normalizers to retain tie uncertainty at
large common strength offsets. Subtracting absolute log normalizers can erase
that uncertainty even when each normalizer is computed with log-sum-exp.

Completion uses the model's geometric next-lap failure and right-censored
exposure likelihood. Each entrant's exposure must be supplied explicitly within
the scheduled distance. The generator samples failures within that window and
otherwise reports censoring at its end. It does not derive an exposure from a
future observed result. Log probability for each completion observation and
conditional running order is kept on the original joint draw; their log factors
are added before any posterior integration.

In particular, a posterior predictive conditional order given an observed running
panel is not generally obtained by just averaging its per-draw order probability.
If the panel is generated by the model, conditioning on it reweights draws by its
probability. Joint order/completion probabilities similarly require averaging
the product of the factors, not multiplying their separate averages. The final
classification-conditioning rule and the comparison with baselines still need
to be frozen in the predictive registration; these kernels do not silently
choose or score that rule.

Random streams attach to seed, event, segment and driver/car identities in a
separate observation namespace. They replay the full small chain/draw stream
before taking a batch slice, including when Student-t degrees of freedom vary
by draw. Changing batch size, input field row order or the length of the future
path therefore does not change earlier draws. Repeating future trajectories and
establishing Monte Carlo precision still require the final replication policy.
The later [race-order generation correction](overall_order_generation.md) also
preserves the sampled Gumbel perturbations when a large common strength would
round them away. It does not add a random draw or replace the existing streams.

## Remaining scientific and production requirements

- Future wetness and neutralisation are conditional inputs here. Their
  forecast-time distribution, source availability and integration remain open;
  unknown conditions cannot default to dry/green or use realized test outcomes.
- An unseen circuit has no registered outcome-coefficient extension yet. The
  iterator rejects it explicitly. The final evaluation must retain every required
  event in its inventory and record that unresolved prediction, not drop the race
  to produce a favorable score.
- Missing qualifying times, penalties and grid eligibility need the complete
  grid-generation policy. The current conditional qualifying positions are not
  an official-grid simulator.
- Fixed exposure does not model lapping, shortened races, stoppages or correlated
  incidents. The common all-cause hazard still does not distinguish mechanical
  opportunity from driver mistakes. Final classification and points require
  those unresolved assumptions and their validation.
- The canonical equal-opportunity producer still needs the accepted common
  scenario, recovery, calibration, predictive evidence, refresh and deployment
  checks. Observational totals for the announced cars and teams are not the
  counterfactual driver ranking.

## Verification

[`test_overall_observation_kernels.py`](../tests/test_overall_observation_kernels.py)
checks qualifying observation densities against a joint model trace with matched
innovations, and circuit/condition coefficients and race likelihood factors
against the existing probability model. Exhaustive small-panel permutations and
geometric censoring sums have known normalization. Additional checks cover shared
teammate effects, elimination, extreme logits, generated order/completion
frequencies, batching, field permutation, path prefixes and input rejection.

These are software and generative-equivalence checks on synthetic inputs. They
cannot establish convergence, predictive calibration, cross-team identification
or a driver's rank. The numerical-study proposal remains pending human approval.
The [verification record](../outputs/championship/development/overall-observation-checks/verification.json)
identifies the committed source, passing focused and full-suite reports, preserved
test-fixture failures and unchanged fitting-input identity.
The later [race-order verification](../outputs/championship/development/overall-order-numerics-checks/verification.json)
records the relative-normalizer correction, its failing-before/passing-after
regressions, unchanged original synthetic inputs and fresh software/resource
checks. That correction changes the fitting-input identity; earlier receipts
remain evidence only for their recorded source.

The [resource profiler](../analysis/overall_forecast_resources.py) can also
exercise these conditional observations after loading a temporary compressed
archive of synthetic parameters at the committed training-data shape. It retains
only output checksums, coverage and measurements. It uses hypothetical conditions
and an unchanged future field, and does not measure fitting or verify the intended
CI runner. Its receipt is explicitly a cache-format fixture, not a successful
convergence result. The [local resource record](../outputs/championship/development/overall-observation-checks/resources.json)
confirms complete draw coverage and finite conditional outputs within the
enforced scope limits.
