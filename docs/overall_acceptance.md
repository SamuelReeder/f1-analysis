# Overall acceptance evidence controller

The acceptance layer must compute the decisions behind the public contract from
registered, matching evidence. A source fingerprint authenticates an identity;
it does not turn a caller-supplied success flag into a scientific result. Passing
one check does not authorize publication or supply another missing check.

The first implemented evaluator independently checks final-fit convergence.
Recovery, chronological prediction, calibration, independent prospective evidence,
functional simulation precision and historical source availability remain
separate requirements. Their incomplete definitions must be completed before
their scientific runs, with the original failures and stopping rules preserved.

## Complete convergence evidence

Use the registered production inference implementation, its exact training
arrays and entrant catalog, and a source-matched posterior. Validate the fit
registration, study, provenance role, fixed settings and complete retry sequence.
The fit must retain every completed attempt and its sampler extras. A failed or
incomplete fit cannot become a posterior cache through this evaluator.

Derive the retained sample and deterministic-site inventory from the actual
composed model. A fixed prior trace supplies shapes and dtypes only; no MCMC is
run. The receipt and archive must contain exactly that model inventory. A
caller-supplied list, repeated in a receipt, cannot omit an inconvenient
parameter, add a substitute or change its shape. Compare this inventory with
the actual sampler collector in software verification.

Read every posterior field through bounded temporary memory maps. Recompute
the unchanged parameter diagnostics and every current-driver qualifying/race
contrast using all chain/draw coordinates. Recount divergence flags, tree-depth
saturation and mean acceptance probability from the saved sampler extras.
Compare the new reports with the recorded reports. Parameter convergence,
driver-contrast convergence and report agreement are all required for this
evidence check to pass. Diagnostic row ordering carries no scientific meaning;
parameter rows may be normalized by name for comparison. No draw or failure may
be discarded.

This evaluator checks the final accepted-by-the-fitter attempt. Earlier attempts
remain in the registered sequence and their sampler-extra checks still apply;
their full parameter reports are not independently recomputed by this stage.
The distinction must remain explicit in its output.

## Artifact and decision boundaries

An evidence directory is immutable. Source files and the definition must be
committed before evaluation. The saved report binds the fit, posterior, receipt,
registration, model inventory and evaluator sources. Check source, data,
registration and archive identities through successful exit. A changed or
incomplete input produces a preserved exception record. An independently failed
diagnostic produces a complete report with a failed decision. Neither permits
another fit attempt or a changed threshold.

Independent convergence is necessary and insufficient. It does not establish
correct posterior calibration, cross-team identification, forecast quality,
historical input availability or functional expected-point/rank precision. In
particular, passing a checker on noisy conditional simulation means cannot set
the functional-precision check. The [full plan](overall_model_plan.md) and
[public contract](../contracts/overall.json) retain all their required evidence.
This module grants no publication authority and advances no dashboard pointer.
